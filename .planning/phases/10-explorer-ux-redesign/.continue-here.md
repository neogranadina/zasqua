<context>
The curated graph JSON (data/curated-entity-graph.json) has 100 nodes and 1428 edges with ForceAtlas2 positions. The coordinates span thousands of units (x: -8541 to 2239, y: -18092 to 349). Force-graph treats 1 unit ≈ 1 pixel at zoom 1, so the graph appears as a tiny cluster. The fundamental fix is either: (a) normalise coordinates in the precompute script to fit ~600 unit range, OR (b) adapt the entity.js rendering approach which uses a live simulation with zoomToFit. The entity.js approach is proven — it uses cooldownTime(5000), d3AlphaDecay(0.02), custom d3Force settings, and zoomToFit(0, 30) after 300ms.
</context>
