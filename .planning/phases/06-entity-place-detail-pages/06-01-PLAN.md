**places.js — enrich with _linked_count:**

**descriptions.js — attach _entity_codes and _place_codes:**

After the existing enrichment loop (which attaches `_ancestors` and `_repo`), load the reverse-lookup files:

Then in the description enrichment loop, add:
